Built without PyTorch, TensorFlow, or any ML library, just NumPy. I wrote this project during my engineering studies 5 years ago to get a deeper, hands-on understanding of the mathematics behind neural networks: linear algebra, calculus, and the chain rule all the way down. Finally committing it to GitHub, because why not.
It implements a multilayer fully-connected neural network from the ground up, including weight initialization, activation functions, loss computation, and backpropagation. Designed for anyone who wants to understand what actually happens under the hood of modern deep learning frameworks.
